Studio recording equipment

Several equipment and tools are required for music to be produced. The following are some of the basic equipment and tools used in music production:

(a) Computer: This is a device into which specific software for music production is installed. After installing the software, a music producer can use the computer to edit, mix and master recorded music. Figure 7.4 shows an example of a computer that can be used for music production.

Figure 7.4: A computer that can be used for music production

(b) Keyboard MIDI controller: The term ‘MIDI’ stands for Musical Instrument Digital Interface. A keyboard MIDI controller does not usually create or produce sound by itself without being connected to the computer. When it is connected to a computer, the keys can be played, and the computer will convert what is played into sound. Figure 7.5 shows an example of a keyboard MIDI controller.
