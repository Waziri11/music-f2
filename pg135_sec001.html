(c) Recording: This is the stage where the actual sound is captured. Vocals and instruments are recorded separately or together, depending on the music style and preferences of the producer. This is one of the most critical stages in the music production process, as it forms the base of the final product.

(d) Editing: During this stage, the producer makes necessary changes to the recorded tracks. This may include adjusting, cutting, adding or removing certain parts to improve the structure and clarity of the song. The goal is to make the song clean and more pleasant to listen to.

(e) Mixing: During the mixing stage, different parts of the recorded vocals and instruments are combined. At this stage, the producer adjusts the volume, adds various sound effects and makes sure everything sounds good together.

(f) Mastering: This is the final stage of music production. It is the stage where adjustments are made to the mixed song to achieve maximum sound quality across different listening environments. The song is polished, its volume is adjusted and the sound is improved to make it suitable for distribution. After mastering, the song is ready to be shared through various media outlets such as radio and television stations or the Internet.
