(v) From note A to B = Tone; however, since the pattern for natural minor scales requires you to count a half step or semitone (S), you should decrease the distance between note A and B. This can be done by lowering note B using a flat (♭) sign to get a half step or semitone. Therefore, you will count from note A to B♭ = Semitone (S).
(vi) From note B♭ to C = Tone (T)
(vii) From note C to D = Tone (T)

The order of the D natural minor scale obtained is D-E-F-G-A-B♭-C-D. Figures 2.38, 2.39 and 2.40 show the D natural minor scale constructed on a piano and staff.
