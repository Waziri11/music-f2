Chapter Seven

Music production

Introduction

Music production is now becoming a major career for many Tanzanian youths. In this chapter, you will learn the concept of music production, the structure of a music recording studio, basic stages of the music production process and job opportunities related to music production. The competencies developed will enable you to describe the main parts of a music recording studio and recognise the purpose of common studio equipment and tools. You will also be able to plan simple music production sessions and assess personal interests and talents in relation to music production careers.

The concept of music production

Music production is a process in which music is created, recorded and preserved to be distributed to the users. The produced music can either be instrumental, vocal or both. In music production, producers employ technology to capture and process musical sounds to produce the desired music. The process of music production can either be done in a music studio or a live performance setting. At this level, you will learn about studio music production.
