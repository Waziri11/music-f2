The ‘A’ natural minor scale

You can arrange a series of eight notes starting from the first note, which is ‘A,’ when constructing the ‘A’ natural minor scale. This can be done while following the pattern for constructing natural minor scales, which is T-S-T-T-S-T-T. The following are steps that can be followed when constructing the ‘A’ natural minor scale:

(a) Step 1: Write a series of eight notes from note A.

A-B-C-D-E-F-G-A

(b) Step 2: Count the steps between the notes while following the pattern T-S-T-T-S-T-T as follows:

(i) From note A to B = Tone (T)

(ii) From note B to C = Semitone (S)

(iii) From note C to D = Tone (T)

(iv) From note D to E = Tone (T)

(v) From note E to F = Semitone (S)

(vi) From note F to G = Tone (T)

(vii) From note G to A = Tone (T)

The order of the ‘A’ natural minor scale obtained is A-B-C-D-E-F-G-A. Figures 2.36 and 2.37 show the ‘A’ natural minor scale constructed on a piano and staff.
