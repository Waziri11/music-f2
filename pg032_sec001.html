Activity 2.2

Find a piece of music written on the grand staff where both staffs use the same clef.

Carefully look at the notes on each staff.

Then, answer the following questions:

  1. (a) How many independent voices are used in the piece?
  2. (b) Which clef is used in both staffs?
  3. (c) Which voice is written on the upper staff and which on the lower staff?
  4. (d) Why do you think the composer used the particular clef on both staffs?
