Music for Secondary Schools Student’s Book Form Two

For a choir with a large number of singers, for example twenty-four singers, they can be arranged into two rows. Each row will consist of twelve singers. In the front row, sopranos will be positioned on the left followed by altos on the right. In the back row, tenors will be positioned on the left, behind sopranos, followed by bass on the right behind altos. Note that singers in the front row must form a line that gives space for those in the back row to see the conductor and the audience.
