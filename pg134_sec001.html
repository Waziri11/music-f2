Basic stages of the music production process

There are several stages that a producer follows in music production. The following are key stages that can be followed when producing a song:

(a) Creating a musical idea: This is the initial stage in which the song’s concept is developed. It involves writing a song and getting it ready for recording. It is also possible for a producer to create instrumental music and give it to a singer(s) to sing along to.

(b) Rehearsing the singer(s) on the prepared instrumental music: At this stage, the producer works with the singer(s) to rehearse using the instrumental track that was created. Rehearsals help the performers become familiar with the music and understand how to deliver their vocals effectively during recording. This also helps in identifying areas for improvement before the actual recording begins.
