Glossary

Amplitude: the greatest distance that a wave, especially a sound or radio wave, vibrates (moves up and down)
Blurry: without a clear outline; not clear
Heritage: the history, traditions, buildings, and objects that a country or society has had for many years and that are considered an important part of its character
Hybrid: something that is the product of mixing two or more different things
Instrumental track: a piece of music (usually popular music) in which only musical instruments are used, with no singing
March: a piece of music with a strong, regular beat, written for marching to
Messy: dirty and/or untidy
Meter: a device that measures and records the amount of electricity, gas, water, etc., that you have used or the time and distance you have travelled
Mouthpiece: the part of a musical instrument that you place between your lips
Musical extract: a short portion or segment taken from a large musical work
Musical score: written or printed version of a piece of music showing what each instrument is to play or what each voice is to sing
Percussion: musical instruments that you play by hitting them with your hand or with a stick, for example drums
Percussionist: person who plays percussion instruments
Reverb: sound effect that can be changed by electronic means to give music more or less of an echo
