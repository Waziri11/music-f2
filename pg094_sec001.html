Vocal ensemble

Vocal ensemble performances are an important part of music, bringing together multiple singers to create a rich and harmonious sound. Singing in an ensemble requires teamwork since singers must blend their voices, stay in tune, and keep the rhythm together to create a smooth and unified sound. Different cultures have their own unique traditions of vocal ensemble singing, with special techniques and styles that make each performance different. In different societies, vocal ensemble performances are a powerful way to tell stories, express emotions, and bring people together through music.

Principles of singing in a vocal ensemble

(a) Understanding each part of the song to be sung: An ensemble singer should understand each part of the song before the performance. Apart from participating in ensemble rehearsals, individual singers can go through the tune, words, musical terms and signs, and other parts of the song over and over on their own time before getting to the performance. This will increase confidence to singers and make them have good performance.
(b) Singing in tune: This simply means to accurately sing the correct notes of a musical piece. When singing in an ensemble, it is important for all singers to sing in tune to produce sounds that are in agreement. Proper voice production will enable a singer to sing in tune.
(c) Singing while carefully listening to each other: This guides singers to avoid singing differently from others. It ensures that no singer in an ensemble is either louder or softer than others. It also helps to make sure that no one is in a different tune or tempo. This will make the voices of the whole ensemble blend well and sound pleasantly.
(d) Paying a close attention to the conductor and follow the instructions: During performance in an ensemble, singers should always keep their eyes to the conductor to receive various instructions and respond together as a team. For example, the conductor will give a sign when to start or end a song, or will do any action based on the song message being communicated to the audience. A singer who may go astray during performance can adjust oneself by paying attention to the conductor.
