Music for Secondary Schools Student’s Book Form Two

  1. (a) Step 1: Write a series of eight notes from note B♭.
    B♭-C-D-E-F-G-A-B♭
  2. (b) Step 2: Count the steps between the notes while following the pattern T-T-S-T-T-T-S as follows:
    1. (i) From note B♭ to C = Tone (T)
    2. (ii) From note C to D = Tone (T)
    3. (iii) From note D to E = Tone; however, since the pattern for major scales requires you to count a half step or semitone (S), you have to decrease the distance between note D and E. This can be done by lowering note E using a flat (♭) sign to get a half step or semitone. Therefore, you will count from note D to E♭ = Semitone (S).
    4. (iv) From note E♭ to F = Tone (T)
    5. (v) From note F to G = Tone (T)
    6. (vi) From note G to A = Tone (T)
    7. (vii) From note A to B♭ = Semitone (S)

Therefore, the order of the B♭ major scale obtained will be B♭-C-D-E♭-F-G-A-B♭. Figures 2.22, 2.23 and 2.24 show a B♭ major scale constructed on a piano and staff.
