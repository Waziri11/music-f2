Understanding musical instruments

Music is an essential part of human culture, and musical instruments are the tools that bring it to life. They are tools used to create sound and express music. There are various types of musical instruments in different shapes, sizes and materials. Each instrument produces unique sounds based on how they are played. Some instruments produce sound by plucking strings, others by blowing air and some by hitting the surface of an instrument.
