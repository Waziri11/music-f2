Parts of piano

It is important to know different parts of the piano and their functions. Knowledge about the parts and functions helps to understand how the piano works and how to play it well. Each part of the piano plays a special role in making music. The main parts of the piano include the following:

(i)

Keyboard: A board with black and white keys that produce different tones when pressed.

(ii)

The fallboard: It is the part of a piano that usually folds down over the keys to cover and protect the keyboard when the piano is not in use. It keeps the keys safe from dust, dirt and accidental damage.

(iii)

The piano lid: The top cover of the piano that can be opened to increase sound projection or closed to protect the instrument.

(iv)

Music rack: It holds sheet music or books in place so that the pianist can read and play at the same time. It is usually located just above the keyboard and can often be adjusted or folded down when not in use.

(v)

Pedals: They are found at the bottom of the piano. They help control the length and quality of the sound. The right pedal (sustain pedal) makes the notes keep sounding even after the player lifts his or her fingers from the keys. This helps the music sound smooth and connected. The middle pedal (sostenuto pedal) holds only the notes that are being played when the pedal is pressed, while other notes played later will not be sustained. The left pedal (soft pedal) makes the sound softer and gentler.

(vi)

Strings and hammers: These are found inside the piano. When the keys are pressed, the harmers strike the strings to produce sound.
