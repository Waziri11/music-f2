The ‘A’ major scale

When constructing the ‘A’ major scale, you can arrange a series of eight notes starting from the first note, which is ‘A’. This can be done while following the pattern for constructing major scales, which is T-T-S-T-T-T-S. The following are steps that can be followed when constructing the ‘A’ major scale:

(a) Step 1: Write a series of eight notes from note A.

A-B-C-D-E-F-G-A

(b) Step 2: Count the steps between the notes while following the pattern T-T-S-T-T-T-S as follows:

(i) From note ‘A’ to B = Tone (T)

(ii) From note B to C = Semitone; however, since the pattern for major scales requires you to count the whole step or tone (T), you have to increase the distance between note B and C. This can be done by raising note C using a sharp (♯) sign to get the whole step or tone. Therefore, you will count from note B to C♯ = Tone (T).

(iii) From note C♯ to D = Semitone (S)

(iv) From note D to E = Tone (T)

(v) From note E to F = Semitone; however, since the pattern for major scales requires you to count the whole step or tone (T), you have to increase the distance between note E and F. This can be done by raising note F using a sharp (♯) sign to get the whole step or tone. Therefore, you will count from note E to F♯ = Tone (T).

(vi) From note F♯ to G = Semitone; however, since the pattern for major scales requires you to count the whole step or tone (T), you have to increase the distance between note F♯ and G. This can be done by raising note G using a sharp (♯) sign to get the whole step or tone. Therefore, you will count from note F♯ to G♯ = Tone (T).

(vii) From note G♯ to A = Semitone (S)
