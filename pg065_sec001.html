Terms and signs indicating tempo

These are terms and signs that indicate the speed of music; that is, how fast or slow certain music should be performed. Tempo can be indicated using Italian terms or with a specific number of beats per minute (BPM). Musicians normally use a metronome to practise staying in tempo and maintaining a consistent rhythm. A metronome is a device that produces a regular and steady beat at a set tempo. However, there are digital metronomes that can be found as applications in digital devices, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops and so forth. The following are examples of terms and signs that indicate tempo:
