(a) Finding inspiration: The first step in a songwriting is finding inspiration. Inspiration can come from personal experiences, emotions, nature, books or even other songs. Many songwriters start by jotting down ideas or recording melodies that come to mind.

(b) Choosing the song structure: A well-structured song has different sections, including the verse, chorus and bridge. The right time to decide on the structure of a song depends on the songwriter’s creative process. Some songwriters establish the structure early, especially if they follow a common format like verse-chorus-verse-chorus-bridge-chorus. Others allow the song to evolve naturally, shaping the structure after developing the melody or lyrics.

(c) Creating the melody: Once an idea is formed and the song structure is identified, the next step is to create the melody. The melody should be catchy, memorable and reflect the song’s mood. Before deciding on the most suitable melody for the song, composers often try out various notes and rhythms. Playing an instrument like a guitar or piano can help in creating the melody. At this stage, the composer can develop a melody for a verse, chorus and bridge.

(d) Writing the lyrics: Lyrics are important for telling a story, expressing emotions or conveying a particular message through the song. Some composers start with lyrics before composing a melody, while others fit the lyrics into the existing melody. In our case, since we already have the melody, this stage will involve preparing the lyrics and fitting them into the existing melody. Composers often rewrite lyrics multiple times to ensure they fit well with the melody and maintain a smooth flow.

(e) Arranging the song: After composing the melody and lyrics, the next step is arranging the song. This involves deciding which instruments will be used and how different sections will be played. At this stage, a songwriter may get assistance from a producer or song arranger.

(f) Refining and finalising the song: At this stage, songwriters review and refine their work, making changes to improve the song’s structure, melody or lyrics. They listen to the song multiple times to identify areas that need adjustment. Some songs go through multiple drafts before they are finalised. After the song is refined, a songwriter may perform the song live or work in a studio for professional production.
