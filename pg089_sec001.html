Chapter Six

Musical performances

Introduction

Musical performance is more than entertainment; it is a primary medium for musicians to communicate with society. In this chapter, you will learn about songwriting, ensemble performances and be introduced to piano playing. The competencies developed will enable you to create original songs with meaningful lyrics and melodies, perform alone and with others in a group and use the piano to accompany short songs.

Songwriting

Songwriting is the process of making a song by creating lyrics, melodies and musical structure for a song. Songwriters put together different parts like verses, choruses and bridges to build a complete and interesting song. People write songs for different purposes including personal expression, professional performance and others. A well-written song not only sounds interesting but also tells a story, conveys emotions and connects deeply with listeners. Songwriting has the power to move and inspire, leaving a lasting impact in peoples’ hearts and minds. It is not just about words and tunes; it also needs good structure, smooth flow and real emotion to make the song special and unforgettable.
